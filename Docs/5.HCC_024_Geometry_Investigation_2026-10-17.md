The sampled CT slice positions progress at 2.5 mm intervals. The SEG positions are consistently 1 mm higher in Z than their explicitly referenced CT positions.

## 5. Interpretation and limitations

The investigation establishes a systematic 1 mm through-plane position discrepancy despite matching Frame of Reference UID, orientation, and pixel spacing.

The available evidence does not establish the cause. The observed discrepancy could not be explained by an explicit registration transform in the inspected SEG metadata. The matching slice thickness does not, by itself, justify changing the SEG positions.

The investigation has not established whether the discrepancy originated during annotation, export, or another stage of data preparation. No such cause should be assumed.

## 6. Decision

- Do not classify HCC_024 as training-ready while the discrepancy remains unresolved.
