# HCC_089: Declared Source versus Geometric Candidate

Date: 2026-10-17

## Purpose

Document the discrepancy between the SEG-declared source CT series and the alternative CT series that shows stronger geometric agreement.

## Declared Source

- The SEG contains 380 frames.
- Its nested reference structure contains five occurrences of ReferencedSOPInstanceUID, all representing one unique CT SOP Instance UID.
- The referenced CT SOP exists locally.
- The referenced instance belongs to series 1.3.6.1.4.1.14519.5.2.1.1706.8374.187699732728809609459703673419.
- Its Series Description is Recon 3: LIVER 2PHASE CAP.
- The referenced CT and SEG share the same Study UID and Frame of Reference UID.

## Geometry Audit Findings

The existing HCC_089 geometry summary reports:

| Candidate | Frames within 1 mm | Tumor-positive frames within 1 mm |
|---|---:|---:|
| RECON3_LIVER_2PHASE (declared source series) | 4 of 380 | 0 of 15 |
| RECON2_LIVER_2PHASE (alternative candidate) | 316 of 380 | 15 of 15 |
| RECON2_PRE (alternative candidate) | 380 of 380 | 15 of 15 |

For RECON2_LIVER_2PHASE, all 15 tumor-positive frames have exact plane matches, full mask-pixel containment within the CT grid, and zero measured in-plane origin offsets.

These geometry findings do not establish that either alternative series is the authoritative source for the SEG.

## Interpretation

HCC_089 has a provenance-versus-geometry conflict. The declared source series has poor geometric plane coverage, while an alternative series provides strong geometric agreement for the tumor-positive frames. The SEG's sparse declared reference structure does not authorize silently replacing the source series.

## Decision

- Preserve the original SEG and CT files.
- Do not replace source references or shift/resample masks.
- Keep HCC_089 excluded from the primary cohort pending mapping review.
- Do not treat geometric alignment alone as proof of source identity.
- Reassess eligibility only after authoritative provenance and annotation quality are reviewed.

## Remaining Work

1. Seek authoritative dataset documentation or provenance explaining the source-series mismatch.
2. Review annotation quality and coverage.
3. Update the eligibility manifest only after documented review.
