# HCC_019 Source-Series and Geometry Investigation

Date: 2026-10-17

## Purpose

Investigate the 25 mm in-plane geometry discrepancy reported for HCC_019 without modifying the original SEG, CT DICOM files, or historical audit outputs.

## Findings

- The SEG contains 268 frames, and all 268 have locally resolved source CT references.
- The 268 declared frame references resolve to 67 unique SOP Instance UIDs in the currently referenced CT series.
- The current referenced CT series has matching Frame of Reference UID, orientation, and pixel spacing, but every frame has a consistent 25 mm Y-position discrepancy.
- Direct comparison confirms CT-minus-SEG position difference of approximately (0, +25, 0) mm across all 268 frame-reference comparisons.
- A second local CT series has positions matching all 268 SEG frame planes within 0.1 mm, with a maximum nearest-plane distance of approximately 0.00001 mm.
- The alternative series is labelled "Recon 2: LIVER 3 PHASE (AP)" and ImageType includes ORIGINAL, PRIMARY, AXIAL. The currently referenced series is labelled "LIVER 3 PHASE (AP)" and ImageType includes ORIGINAL, SECONDARY, AXIAL.
- Both series belong to the same patient and study and share the same Frame of Reference UID.
- The SEG's declared source SOP references point to the current series, not the alternative series.
- The two series have different pixel arrays at all 67 shared Z positions.
- A diagnostic row-shift correlation search across 67 slices found a median best shift of 34 rows, with a range of 30–36 rows and median correlation of approximately 0.938193.

## Interpretation

The alternative series is geometrically aligned with the SEG frame positions and has strongly correlated image content with the currently referenced series. However, it is not pixel-identical, and its relationship to the SEG's intended source data has not been established.

The correlation analysis is diagnostic only. It does not validate a spatial transformation, prove pixel-level correspondence, or establish that the alternative series is the intended source for the annotation.

## Decision

- Preserve the SEG's original source references and all original DICOM files.
- Do not shift, resample, or rewrite the SEG mask or DICOM metadata.
- Do not silently substitute the alternative CT series.
- Keep HCC_019 excluded from the primary cohort pending source-series provenance review and annotation quality review.
- Revisit eligibility only when defensible provenance or authoritative dataset documentation resolves the mapping.

## Remaining Work

1. Review authoritative dataset documentation or source provenance for the relationship between the primary reconstruction and secondary series.
2. If provenance remains unresolved, retain the exclusion rather than infer a correction from geometric alignment or correlation.
3. Complete annotation QC and update the eligibility manifest only after review.
