### 6. Required gates before training

1. Resolve source-series and spatial-mapping questions for pending mapping cases.
2. Complete and document formal annotation QC, including source-series verification and boundary review where applicable.
3. Resolve spatial-provenance exceptions and incomplete source-reference coverage.
4. Freeze and version the final cohort eligibility manifest.
5. Verify preprocessing, train/validation/test separation, and leakage controls before training.
6. Reconfirm environment and GPU configuration when Phase 3 begins.

Do not silently relax geometry thresholds, shift masks, edit DICOM identifiers, or promote pending cases solely to increase cohort size.

### 7. Phase 3 readiness

**Phase 3 model training is not authorized by this report.**

The project can proceed with implementation planning and non-training preparation. Training on HCC-TACE-Seg cases should wait until each selected case satisfies the documented source-mapping, spatial-validation, and annotation-QC criteria.

The planned study arms remain:

- Scratch baseline.
- CT-ORG liver-only pretraining.
- CT-ORG multi-organ pretraining.

The intended evaluation includes Dice, HD95, precision/recall, lesion-size stratification, and domain robustness/reliability. These are planned experiments, not completed results.

### 8. Closure statement

The Phase 2 audit and documentation cycle is closed for the current review. The dataset remains **NOT ESTABLISHED AS TRAINING-READY**: 105 of 105 patients lack established training readiness. Outstanding eligibility and QC gates carry forward as explicit prerequisites for Phase 3 training.

No original DICOM files, masks, source manifests, or existing QC reports were intentionally modified by preparing this closure report.
