## Purpose

Document the common slice-normal geometry discrepancy identified in the existing HCC SEG-to-CT frame-reference audit.

| Patient | Audit records | Unique frames | Normal offset | Maximum in-plane difference |

All records have locally resolved source references. The existing exception audit reports matching patient, series, Frame of Reference UID, orientation and pixel spacing for these cases.

The detailed audit reports a consistent +1.0 mm normal-direction difference for every record. In-plane differences are negligible relative to the slice-normal discrepancy.

## Interpretation and Limitations

These three cases form a consistent geometry-review category. The audit establishes the numerical pattern but does not establish its cause, the intended annotation-to-image plane relationship, or whether a correction is appropriate.

A consistent offset must not automatically be interpreted as a safe mask translation.

## Decision

- Preserve original CT and SEG DICOM files.
- Do not shift, resample or rewrite masks or DICOM metadata.
- Retain the existing geometry-review status pending provenance review.
- Reassess primary-cohort eligibility only after the offset's origin and annotation semantics are adequately supported.

## Remaining Work

1. Investigate the source of the systematic offset using available authoritative provenance and acquisition metadata.
2. Complete annotation quality review.
3. Update the eligibility manifest only after review and documented approval.
