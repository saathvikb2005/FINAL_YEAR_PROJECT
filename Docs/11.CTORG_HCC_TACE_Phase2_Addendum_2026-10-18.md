## 5. Required gates before Phase 3 training

1. Resolve or explicitly adjudicate outstanding source mapping, Frame of Reference, and spatial-offset exceptions using documented evidence.
2. Complete formal annotation QC under a recorded protocol, including explicit acceptance criteria and reviewer decisions.
3. Correct and verify any errors in screening notes without changing original source annotations.
4. Produce a final patient-level eligibility manifest with evidence-backed inclusion, exclusion, and unresolved-case decisions.
5. Freeze label mapping, preprocessing, resampling, and geometry-handling rules.
6. Define patient-level train/validation/test splits after eligibility is established, avoiding patient leakage.
7. Verify the training environment and resource limits before selecting feasible model configurations.
8. Produce a Phase 2 closure report that records completed gates, outstanding limitations, and the authorized eligible cohort.

## 6. Data integrity safeguards

Do not infer missing source references from proximity, silently substitute CT series, alter Frame of Reference identifiers, or shift masks merely to make geometry checks pass. Preserve source files and prior audit artifacts. Any approved derived mapping must be documented separately and reproducible.

## Final decision

**Phase 2 remains open. Training readiness is not established for any of the 105 patients.** Phase 3 model training should wait until eligibility, annotation QC, and preprocessing rules are documented and approved.
